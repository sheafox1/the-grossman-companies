How It Works

From loan request to funded draw

Our lending team underwrites, closes and services every loan in house, so you deal with the same people from start to payoff.

1. Send your project

Share the address, purchase price, construction budget and your exit plan.

2. Term sheet

We value the asset in house and send a term sheet with every fee clearly disclosed.

3. Close once title is clear

Loans can close in as little as 1 to 2 weeks. No committee meets weekly to hold you up.

4. Draws in 24 hours

Request a draw, we inspect and check title, and funds are often wired within 24 hours.
